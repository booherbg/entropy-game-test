# LOOPHOLE — Holistic Evaluation (fractal pass)

**Date:** 2026-06-16 · **Method:** simulate (harness + probes), read the systems at two
scales — *minor* (numbers, edges, legibility) and *mega* (economy, arc, theme, modes) — channel
the murmurs (the game's own thesis) as the design rubric. Findings are evidence-backed; actions
taken this session are marked ✓, open watch-items marked ◦.

The rubric the murmurs set: *life is a loophole — local order bought from the gradient, billed back
by the second law; complexity bootstraps on energy flow; we are here to witness it.* Mechanics
should embody that. Where they don't, that's the gap.

## Mega — the whole

### 1. The economy interlocks (good), with one strong faucet
Three currencies form a real loop:
- **✦ order** — build capital (income + bloom-births + stage-ups), spent on patterns, rites, tending.
- **❧ sap** — per-turn flow: producers → consumers → surplus → order. The metabolism.
- **✸ insight** — meta-currency: milestones + *heat* (order radiated above the soft cap, spilled/16),
  spent on cultivars and now legendaries.

The loop is sound: **surplus order → heat → insight → rule-benders.** Evidence: a typical win
radiates **~495 order/run** as heat (≈31 insight from heat alone), on top of milestones. That's a
**strong faucet** — which is *fine* now that downstream sinks exist (cultivars, the legendary shop)
and the long game scores against idling. The moss-carpet "spam space" farm is the faucet's failure
mode; it's mitigated three ways (steep legendary pricing, long-game diversity scoring, the planned
diminishing-returns backstop). ◦ Keep watching it.

### 2. The base game converges on a narrow optimum
The winning line is **moss + frond + ant + mycelium + heartwood + every cultivar**, and it wins
24/24 in ~46 turns. But probes show two patterns falling out of that optimum:
- **blooms: present in 0/12 winning runs** (see Minor #1 — fixed at the root this pass),
- **legendaries: 0 bought/run** (see Minor #2 — intended, but worth a human check).

Mega read: the base (awaken-toward-coherence) game **doesn't reward diversity or build identity** —
the two things the macro vision is *built on*. This isn't a bug; it's the ceiling of the base game,
and it's exactly why the roadmap exists. The fixes are already pointed at it: **the shop** (build
identity via rule-benders), **the long game** (diversity *is* the score), and eventually **fauna**
(diversity *is* the content). The base game is the tutorial for an ecology it can't yet show.

### 3. The two modes are well-differentiated, and the long game's *measure* is its design
- **the garden** — one awakening, ~46-turn arc, the debuggable baseline (unchanged this session).
- **the long game** — no single waking; scored at turn 100 on *what it sustains* (coherence, scale,
  **diversity ×45/kind**, blossoms, network, disorder devoured). Because the score weights diversity
  and life, the moss-carpet idle scores *low* — the mode's own measure rejects the exploit and
  rewards a real ecology. ✓ This is the thesis made into a number.

### 4. Theme manifestation is strong and improving
moss cleaning entropy at the frontier (✓ fixed this pass to actually advance on the gradient),
ants as Maxwell's demon turning disorder into insight-fragments (✓ wired to the shop), heat as the
second law billing held order, the awakening as witnessing. The biggest *unrealized* theme is
**emergence** — blooms are the only self-replicating CA, and they were vestigial. Fixing blooms (✓)
and the propagation/fauna arc (◦) is how emergence becomes felt, not just narrated.

## Minor — the details

1. ✓ **Blooms were deadweight.** `_sap` gave a standing bloom `out: 0` — 0.45 sap in, nothing out.
   Now a fed blossom pays a small order that grows with age (bed of 10 ≈ 2.8 order/turn), upkeep held
   at 0.45 so it's an investment, not free order. *Full bed viability still needs the sustain work.*
2. ◦ **Legendaries near-unattainable in bot play (0/12).** The shop did its job (expensive), but the
   cultivar-greedy bot never banks enough. A human who *wants* a rule-bender can save for it, and the
   ant relic-fragment discount is a second road. **Verify in human playtest** that a wanted legendary
   is reachable ~once a run; if not, soften the first cost (6 → 5) before touching the curve.
3. ✓ **Moss crawled backward** onto pruned cells (seeded the calmest neighbour). Now frontier-seeking.
4. ✓ **Mycelium-reach "looked broken"** — it worked (2→3), just invisible. The kit page now shows it
   sourced ("mycelium reach +2 ← the patient geode · rhizomorph").
5. ◦ **Long-game survival to 100 may be hard.** The greedy "always widen" bot *dissolves* before
   turn 100 — unchecked expansion collapses (thematically right). Watch that a *careful* human can
   sustain to 100; if too punishing, ease late-stage rim entropy in longgame only.
6. ◦ **Unverified from earlier:** the "thriving 1.67 / starving 0%" tooltip clarity; the occasional
   soundtrack glitch. Neither reproduced this pass.
