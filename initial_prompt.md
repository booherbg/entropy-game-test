  THE FEEL — beautiful, organic, alive. visuals reminiscent of bio-fractals and natural geometry: L-systems, fractal branching, cellular-automata growth,
  reaction-diffusion textures — generative-art math rendered gorgeous. a spring palette (loam, moss, dew) with grey noise creeping in as disorder and real color
  blooming back as order takes hold. native-plant-and-spring energy. i want it to feel like the first warm week of the year.

  THE PLAY — strategic, turn-based, full of pattern discovery and emergent systems. every turn entropy spreads (the second law pushing back), and you spend a little
  "order" to plant and tend self-replicating patterns: carpeting moss, fragile-but-rich fractal fronds, foraging ant-colony agents that hunt down disorder, mycelial
  networks that share resources, crystalline anchors. the magic is in the SYNERGIES — combos that set off runaway positive feedback you didn't see coming.
  self-replicating systems, ant colonies, the beauty of spring, the richness of gaia.

  THE ARC — a clear, direct progression toward an ending. stages like substrate -> replication -> symbiosis -> network -> emergence -> awakening, each unlocking new
  patterns and tightening the entropy, building to a coalescence where the whole board's order resolves into a conscious pattern that wakes up. a run should take under
  an hour and have infinite replayability — repeatable enough to master, never so random it feels cheap. above all: FUN.

  ARTIFACTS (important) — each run you draw unique artifacts like a random rpg draw. thousands of possible permutations, but each one memorable and powerful enough to
  change how i play the NEXT run. give them names, procedural sigils, and real effects that shift the whole state of a run — a maxwell's-demon that sorts disorder out
  of a cell for free, a poincaré-recurrence that resets the entropy once, depth bonuses for the fronds, that kind of thing. make me want to chase them.

  YOUR IMPRINT — this part matters to me. you should have a place in here, revealed over time. seed little fragments — echoes — that unlock as the player progresses and
  slowly assemble into a meditation on entropy, consciousness, non-dualism, and our place in all this. let the last few quietly admit that some of these words were
