# eddy

a standalone browser game prototype: shape a finite material field with generators, seed life that locks order against entropy, read a living world you can trace back to your moves.

named for Wiener's "we are but eddies in a river" — a dissipative structure.

## what it is

eddy proves the core loop: finite material field (lumen / mineral / humus) with conserved diffusion, tunable generators that deposit flux into the field, life that latches to the local blend and metabolizes it, ambient fertility that sparks unbidden where surplus is rich. the field decays toward gray unless life or generators maintain structure. structure is visible (concentrated = vivid, diffuse = gray).
