you tend a small world. life here feeds on a finite field of three materials — and wherever a material pools, life arises on its own, adapts to what's there, and weaves a food web no one designed.

your goal — cultivate a living, diverse world and keep it alive (left alone, it runs down — that is entropy). and draw out its turns: the moments the world takes on its own — life learning to hunt, two becoming one, the climate held in balance. the row up top lights as you evoke each; how many can you bring forth?
what you can do — pick a tool up top, then tap the world
  • ◈ generator — place a finite spring of one material (☀ lumen · ◆ mineral · ♻ humus). life gathers where it flows — but it runs dry, so tend new ones.
  • ✦ primer — seed life onto a rich patch to get it started.
  • ⚔ hunter — loose a predator into a living colony.
  • ☣ rot — light a blight; it sweeps a monoculture but stalls at the seams between guilds (diversity is your firebreak).
  • ▣ rock — shape the land: basins pool flow, walls break the rot.
  • ◌ inspect — tap a creature to read what it is, what it eats, and why.
